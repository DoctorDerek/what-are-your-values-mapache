WAYVM uses a losslessly assembled subset of the 64x64 Default PNG glyphs.
The six-column, eight-row atlas is shared by web and native presentation.
Exact source filenames and atlas columns are recorded in
packages/data/src/ControllerPrompts.ts. Rows follow CONTROLLER_BINDINGS,
followed by the D-pad. No glyph drawing, labels, or colors were modified.

Generic face-position glyphs use the unlettered Nintendo Switch diagrams.
Generic shoulders and center buttons use unbranded L1/R1 and menu/view glyphs.
Controller family is inferred from the input device, never the host OS.
